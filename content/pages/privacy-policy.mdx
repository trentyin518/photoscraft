## 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how PhotoCraft ("PhotoCraft", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, shares, retains and protects personal data when you use our website, photo editor, AI tools and related services (the "Services"). It also explains the rights you have and how to exercise them.

We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy. You can contact us at [support@photoscraft.top](mailto:support@photoscraft.top) or through our [contact page](/contact).

This policy should be read together with our [Terms of Service](/terms), [AIGC Acceptable Use Policy](/aigc) and [Cookie Policy](/cookie).

## 2. Personal Data We Collect

### 2.1 Data you provide

- **Account data**: name, email address, password (stored in hashed form), profile photo and login provider (email, Google or GitHub).
- **Photos and prompts**: the images you upload, the tool and settings you select, any mask you paint, and the images generated from them ("Photo Content").
- **Payment and billing data**: plan, purchase date, amount, currency, invoice and subscription status. Full card details are handled by our payment providers and are not stored by us.
- **Communications**: messages you send to support, feedback you submit, and reports you make.
- **Showcase content**: titles, descriptions, images and comments you choose to publish publicly.

### 2.2 Data collected automatically

- **Usage data**: pages viewed, tools used, job IDs, timestamps, credit balance and actions taken in the editor.
- **Device and log data**: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device type, referring URL, and error and security logs.
- **Cookie data**: as described in our [Cookie Policy](/cookie).

We do not intentionally collect sensitive categories of personal data (such as health, biometric or precise geolocation data). Please do not upload photos or information that you do not need to share.

## 3. How We Use Personal Data

We use personal data to:

- create and manage your account, authenticate you and provide customer support;
- process your photos through the AI model you select and return the generated result;
- manage credits, subscriptions, invoices, refunds and fraud prevention;
- operate, secure, debug and improve the Services, including monitoring reliability and abuse;
- send service messages such as receipts, security alerts and material changes to our terms;
- review and moderate public showcase content and respond to reports;
- comply with legal, tax, accounting and regulatory obligations; and
- establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We do **not** sell your personal data, and we do **not** use your photos to train our own AI models. We do not use your personal data for targeted advertising based on sensitive characteristics.

## 4. Legal Bases (EEA/UK)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

- **Contract**: to provide the Services you request, including processing photos, managing your account, credits and subscriptions.
- **Legitimate interests**: to secure and improve the Services, prevent fraud and abuse, moderate public content, and operate our business, balanced against your rights.
- **Legal obligation**: to keep tax, accounting and other records, and to respond to lawful requests.
- **Consent**: where we ask for it, for example for optional cookies or marketing emails. You may withdraw consent at any time.

## 5. How We Share Personal Data

We share personal data only as described below:

- **AI model and infrastructure providers**: when you run a tool, your image and selected settings are sent to the provider that powers that tool (currently accessed through fal.ai, including Google's Gemini 2.5 Flash Image / Nano Banana, Clarity Upscaler, BiRefNet, rembg and LaMa). These providers process the image to return a result and may apply their own content filters and retention rules.
- **Payment providers**: Stripe, Creem, Waffo or another provider shown at checkout, to process payments, subscriptions, invoices and refunds.
- **Storage and hosting**: cloud storage and hosting providers that store your photos and run the Services.
- **Email and communications**: email delivery providers that send verification, receipt and support messages.
- **Analytics and security**: providers that help us understand usage, monitor errors and prevent abuse, configured to minimise personal data.
- **Legal and safety**: authorities, courts or other parties when required by law, to protect rights and safety, to investigate fraud or abuse, or in connection with a corporate transaction.
- **With your direction**: for example, when you publish content to the public showcase or ask us to share data with a third party.

We require service providers to protect personal data and to process it only for the purposes we specify, except where their own legal obligations apply.

## 6. International Transfers

We and our providers may process personal data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses and equivalent contractual protections. You can contact us for more information about the safeguards used for a particular transfer.

## 7. How Long We Keep Personal Data

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

- **Account data**: while your account is active, and for a short period after deletion to handle backups and disputes.
- **Photo Content**: until you delete the photo, delete the job, or delete your account. Deleting a job removes the input and output URLs from your dashboard; storage objects are deleted on our normal deletion cycle.
- **Billing and tax records**: for as long as required by tax, accounting and consumer-protection law (typically up to 7 years).
- **Support, reports and moderation records**: for up to 3 years after the matter is closed, or longer if needed for legal claims or to prevent repeat abuse.
- **Logs and analytics**: for up to 12 months, unless needed for security investigations.
- **Backups**: deleted data is purged from backups on our normal backup cycle, normally within 30 days.

## 8. Your Data Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights:

- **Access**: obtain a copy of the personal data we hold about you and information about how we use it.
- **Rectification**: ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
- **Erasure**: ask us to delete your personal data, subject to legal retention obligations.
- **Restriction**: ask us to limit how we process your personal data while a dispute is resolved.
- **Objection**: object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time.
- **Portability**: receive personal data you provided in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, or ask us to transmit it to another controller where technically feasible.
- **Withdraw consent**: withdraw any consent you have given, without affecting processing that already took place.
- **Non-discrimination**: we will not deny you the Services or charge you a different price because you exercised your rights.
- **Complaint**: lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. We would appreciate the chance to address your concern first.

### 8.1 How to exercise your rights

- **Access, correction, portability, restriction or objection**: email [support@photoscraft.top](mailto:support@photoscraft.top) with the subject "Data rights request" and describe your request. We may ask you to verify your identity before we act.
- **Correction of profile data**: you can update your name, profile photo and other account details directly in **Settings → Profile**.
- **Deletion of individual photos or jobs**: delete them from your dashboard or email us if a photo cannot be removed there.
- **Account deletion**: go to **Settings → Security → Delete account**. This permanently deletes your account and the associated data described in Section 8.2. You can also email us and we will assist.
- **Authorised agents**: you may use an authorised agent to submit a request on your behalf, subject to verification of your identity and the agent's authority.

We respond to requests within **30 days** of receiving a complete, verified request. If we need more time (for example, for complex or numerous requests), we will tell you within 30 days and may extend the period by up to 30 additional days. We do not charge a fee unless a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

### 8.2 What happens when you delete your account

When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise the personal data associated with it, including your profile, login credentials, photo jobs, input and output image URLs, credit balance, showcase submissions, comments and feedback. Active subscriptions should be cancelled first in **Settings → Billing**; deleting your account does not automatically refund a payment.

Some data may be retained where the law requires it (for example, invoices and tax records for up to 7 years), or where necessary to resolve disputes, prevent fraud or enforce our terms. Residual copies may remain in backups for up to 30 days before being overwritten.

## 9. Security

We use technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data, including encryption in transit, hashed passwords, access controls, restricted storage permissions and logging. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password and notify us promptly if you suspect unauthorised access.

## 10. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are under 13, do not use the Services or provide personal data. If you are between 13 and the age of majority in your jurisdiction, use the Services only with the involvement and consent of a parent or guardian. If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child under 13 without appropriate consent, we will delete it. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us at [support@photoscraft.top](mailto:support@photoscraft.top).

## 11. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies for essential functionality, security, preferences and analytics as described in our [Cookie Policy](/cookie). You can control cookies through your browser settings and, where available, our cookie preferences. Blocking essential cookies may prevent parts of the Services from working.

## 12. Automated Processing and AI

We use automated systems to process images, detect abuse and moderate public content. These systems may make mistakes. We do not use solely automated processing to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you. You can contact us to ask about the logic involved, or to request human review of a moderation decision.

## 13. Third-Party Links

The Services may link to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their privacy policies before providing personal data.

## 14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will post the updated policy on this page, update the date above, and provide additional notice by email or in the Services where appropriate. Your continued use of the Services after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.

For privacy questions, data rights requests, account deletion assistance or complaints, contact us at [support@photoscraft.top](mailto:support@photoscraft.top) or through our [contact page](/contact). If you are in the EEA or UK and are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to your local supervisory authority.
